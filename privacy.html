Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

RTOOS's MVP is built, and we're now onboarding a first group of merchants for pilots. This policy describes how we currently collect and use personal data. It will be updated as the product develops. If you're a merchant considering a pilot, the specific data-processing terms for your account are set out in your Pilot Agreement and Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to RTOOS, a product operated by RIXSTO (proprietor: Ritchie Pulikottil), India. It covers:

  • Merchants (D2C brands) who use RTOOS, and their staff who access it
  • End customers of those merchants, whose order and delivery information may pass through RTOOS as part of order confirmation and RTO/NDR recovery workflows
  • Visitors to our website who submit the early-access form

Where a merchant uses RTOOS to process their customers' data, the merchant is generally the party responsible for collecting that data lawfully and for its relationship with its own customers. RTOOS acts to support the merchant's order-confirmation and delivery-recovery process, as described further below and in the applicable DPA.

2. Information we collect

From merchants and their staff:

  • Account information — name, email, role, organisation
  • Login and session data — password hash, session tokens, login timestamps
  • Usage and audit logs — actions taken within RTOOS

Customer/order information (provided to us by merchants, or entered by their customers during a confirmation flow):

  • Customer phone number
  • Customer name, where provided
  • Customer email, where provided
  • Shipping address
  • Order amount and order details
  • COD status
  • The customer's confirmation or cancellation response

Technical and website data:

  • Basic technical/account data needed to operate RTOOS (e.g. device/browser information tied to a session)
  • Security logs, for detecting abuse and maintaining service integrity
  • If you submit the early-access form on this website, the email address you provide

3. How we use this information

  • To operate the order-confirmation flow: contacting a customer (currently via operator-initiated, prefilled WhatsApp click-to-chat links, or other channels a merchant uses) to confirm or cancel a COD order
  • To apply risk rules to help merchants flag high-risk COD orders
  • To support NDR (non-delivery report) follow-up and recovery workflows
  • To operate merchant accounts — authentication, access control, and audit trails
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the service, including rate limiting and abuse prevention
  • To respond to early-access requests and communicate with prospective merchants
  • To comply with applicable law

We do not currently operate automated or bulk WhatsApp messaging. Where WhatsApp is used, it is via operator-initiated, prefilled click-to-chat links, and RTOOS does not hold Meta/WhatsApp Business API credentials on a merchant's behalf at this stage.

4. Merchant vs. RTOOS responsibilities

In most cases, the merchant determines what customer data is shared with RTOOS and for what purpose (broadly analogous to a "Data Fiduciary" role under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), and RTOOS processes that data to provide the service (broadly analogous to a "Data Processor" role). This general description is not a determination of legal status for every situation — the actual allocation of responsibilities depends on the specific data flows and is set out precisely in that merchant's Pilot Agreement and DPA, which govern in the event of any inconsistency with this general description. Merchants are responsible for having a lawful basis to share their customers' data with us and for their own privacy disclosures to their customers.

5. Service providers

We may use third-party infrastructure and service providers (for example, hosting, code repository, and website/form providers) to operate RTOOS and this website. We do not currently publish a fixed list of subprocessors on this page, as our infrastructure is still evolving during the pilot stage; a current subprocessor list will be provided to merchants as part of their DPA and kept up to date as our infrastructure is finalised.

6. Data retention and deletion

Our default operational rule is: personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the pilot/service and for the purposes described in this policy, and is deleted or returned in accordance with the applicable Pilot Agreement/DPA once a pilot ends or the data is no longer needed — subject to any legal, tax, or security-related retention requirements that require us to keep it longer. Because RTOOS is in an early pilot stage, specific retention periods and deletion timelines for a given merchant are confirmed in that merchant's Pilot Agreement/DPA. Merchants or customers who want data deleted can contact us using the details below, and we will act on reasonable requests in line with applicable law and any obligations to the relevant merchant.

7. Security

The deployed RTOOS application implements password hashing, secure session handling (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite cookies), CSRF protection, rate limiting, protections against account/user enumeration, signed and expiring links for sensitive actions, output escaping, and avoids writing secrets to logs. We also keep merchant data isolated from other merchants at the application level and maintain audit logs of key actions. These are the controls currently in place — RTOOS has not undergone independent security certification (e.g. SOC 2 or ISO 27001) at this stage, and we do not claim otherwise.

8. International data processing

Our infrastructure providers may process or store data outside India depending on where they operate. We do not currently guarantee a specific data location, and this will be clarified as our production infrastructure is finalised and reflected in merchant DPAs. If this changes in a way that's material to how your data is handled, we will update this policy.

9. Your rights and grievances

Depending on applicable law — including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (notified 14 November 2025, with obligations phasing in over the following 18 months) — you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to raise a grievance about how it's handled. We are aligning our practices with the DPDP Act and Rules as their provisions come into force, rather than claiming full compliance with obligations that are not yet in effect. To exercise these rights or raise a concern, contact us using the details in Section 11. Merchants' end customers should also check whether the merchant has its own process for these requests, since the merchant is often the first point of contact for its own customers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as RTOOS develops, particularly as we move from pilot to production. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Material changes affecting active merchants will also be communicated directly where appropriate.
