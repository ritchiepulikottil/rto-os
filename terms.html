1. What RTOOS is

RTOOS is a product operated by RIXSTO (proprietor: Ritchie Pulikottil), aimed at helping Indian D2C brands reduce avoidable COD (cash-on-delivery) order failures — through order confirmation, risk flagging, and NDR (non-delivery) follow-up.
